# Card Update Reminder Policy

## Scope & Applicability
Governs recovery workflows for payment failures caused by expired cards (`card_expired`) or upcoming card expirations on recurring mandates.

## Contact Limits & Timing Schedule
- **Maximum Outreach Limit**: Up to 4 structured reminders across a 14-day window.
- **Pre-expiry Notice (Day -7)**: Send proactive reminder 7 days before card expiry month-end.
- **Touchpoint 1 (Day 1 post-failure)**: Immediate notification informing customer of recurring payment interruption.
- **Touchpoint 2 (Day 5)**: Mid-cycle reminder highlighting upcoming service pause.
- **Touchpoint 3 (Day 12)**: Final notice 48 hours before subscription suspension.

## Channel Hierarchy
- Primary Channel: Email containing tokenized, single-click card update portal.
- Secondary Channel: WhatsApp notification with verified merchant badge.

## Tone Guidance & Security Standards
- Maintain a proactive, helpful, and security-centric tone.
- Never request plain-text card numbers, CVV, or OTP in messages.
- Direct customers exclusively to RBI-compliant, tokenized Razorpay Vault update links.
- Emphasize seamless, uninterrupted continuity of their active subscription or service.
