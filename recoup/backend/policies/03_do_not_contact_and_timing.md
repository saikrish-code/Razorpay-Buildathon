# Do-Not-Contact and Timing Rules

## Permitted Outreach Windows
- **Operational Hours**: Automated customer communication is strictly allowed only between 09:00 IST and 20:00 IST.
- **Quiet Hours**: Absolute communication blackout between 20:01 IST and 08:59 IST. All scheduled triggers during quiet hours must be queued for 09:15 IST the next business day.
- **Restricted Days**: No promotional recovery campaigns on national holidays (Republic Day, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti, Diwali).

## Frequency Capping
- **Global Frequency Limit**: Maximum of 1 outreach message per customer across all channels within any 24-hour rolling window.
- **Cross-Channel Suppression**: If a customer responds or completes payment on WhatsApp, immediately cancel pending SMS and Email jobs.

## Opt-Out & Compliance Protocols
- Customers replying with "STOP", "UNSUBSCRIBE", or "DNC" must be placed on the global Do-Not-Contact list within 60 seconds.
- No further automated outreach may be sent to opted-out customers under penalty of compliance escalation.
- Maintain respectful, professional tone acknowledging customer privacy.
