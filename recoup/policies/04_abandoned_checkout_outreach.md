# Abandoned Checkout Outreach Policy

## Scope & Eligibility
Applies to high-intent shoppers who initiate checkout but drop off before payment completion (`customer_abandoned` or session timeout). Eligible for carts valued >= Rs. 500.

## Engagement Timing & Frequency
- **Maximum Attempts**: Exactly 2 non-intrusive recovery touchpoints.
- **Touchpoint 1 (High-Intent Window)**: Triggered 30 to 45 minutes after abandonment. Captures warm intent while the shopping context is fresh.
- **Touchpoint 2 (24-Hour Reminder)**: Sent exactly 24 hours post-abandonment between 10:00 AM and 18:00 IST, optionally featuring reserved cart availability or free delivery.
- If no action within 48 hours, close the recovery lead automatically.

## Delivery Channels & Experience
- Primary: WhatsApp interactive message displaying cart preview, item thumbnail, and 1-click checkout URL.
- Secondary: Rich SMS with shortened direct Razorpay payment link.

## Tone & Messaging Standards
- Tone must be warm, helpful, concierge-style, and frictionless.
- Example: "Hi [Name], did you leave something behind? Your cart is saved and ready for checkout."
- Avoid aggressive countdown timers or artificial scarcity pressure.
